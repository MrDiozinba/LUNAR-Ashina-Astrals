LUNAR — Data, tools and resource references

NASA DATA / RESOURCES USED IN THE COMPUTATIONAL WORKFLOW
1. NASA Goddard Planetary Geology, Geophysics and Geochemistry Laboratory (PGDA), Site01 high-resolution LOLA terrain product:
   https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/78
   Used for the local Site01 5 m elevation tile and associated product context. Product attribution should retain NASA/GSFC and source metadata as applicable.

2. NASA PGDA, large-area south-polar LOLA terrain products:
   https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/90
   Used for regional 20 m / 80 m LOLA elevation products and multi-resolution terrain coverage.

3. NASA/JPL NAIF SPICE generic kernels and documentation:
   https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/naif/data.html
   https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/generic_kernels/
   Used with the DE440 short planetary ephemeris, lunar orientation/frame kernels, planetary constants kernel and leap-seconds kernel. Record exact kernel filenames and hashes in the project's provenance metadata.

4. JPL Horizons System / API:
   https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/
   https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/horizons.html
   Used for independent apparent azimuth/elevation comparisons against SPICE outputs. The saved validation report records the API version, query parameters and access date.

ATTRIBUTION AND USAGE CAUTIONS
- NASA data and imagery can have product-specific attribution and use terms. Do not imply NASA endorsement of LUNAR.
- Do not use NASA insignia, logotype, seal, flags or mission identifiers as though the project were an official NASA product.
- LROC web-interface imagery/screenshots can have different terms from archived PDS data products. Check the exact data/product licence before redistribution.
- QuickMap and Moon Trek were explored as reference tools; their displayed layers may have individual third-party rights or terms. Credit the underlying data product, not just the viewer.
- Cite the exact PGDA product and kernel version used. Keep raw Horizons query responses and the generated comparison report with the project evidence.
- The video submission guidance requires all other data, resources, tools, code, text and images to be listed, even when open-source or freely available. Confirm any non-NASA terms before redistribution.
